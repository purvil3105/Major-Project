Fig. 13. Comparison of signaling overhead under different (M, A).                               VII. CONCLUSION

                                                                          In this paper, we investigate the joint optimization of access
                                                                       control and resource allocation in the context of random access
                                                                       for LEO satellite IoT systems. To address the challenges
                                                                       of access collisions and resource waste caused by massive
                                                                       device connectivity, we propose a cross-layer joint decision-
                                                                       making framework. At the short-term timescale, matching
                                                                       theory is employed to reduce signaling overhead, while at
                                                                       the long-term timescale, Lyapunov optimization is utilized to
                                                                       achieve dynamic resource scheduling under the constraints of
                                                                       queue stability and average power consumption. The core of
                                                                       the proposed framework is based on the DDPG algorithm,
                                                                       which is used to train and enhance the overall optimization
                                                                       strategy, thereby improving system adaptability and perfor-
                                                                       mance. Simulation results demonstrate that the proposed joint
                                                                       decision-making framework achieves significant performance
                                                                       gains in large-scale access scenarios, effectively mitigating
                                                                       access collisions and resource inefficiencies, and substantially
                                                                       enhancing overall system access efficiency.
