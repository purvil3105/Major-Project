   As illustrated in Fig. 8(c) and (d), the overflight system

This improvement can be attributed to the greater availability
of resources for TDMA scheduling. However, beyond a certain
threshold, further extending TR leads to a decline in through-
put. This degradation occurs because, under a proportional
fair scheduling scheme, UEs with lower data rates occupy
a larger share of the available resources. It is important to
note that for specific values of the number of UEs (NMt ) and
the packet arrival rate (pk), there exists an upper limit to
the achievable system throughput, regardless of the TR value.
Interestingly, our simulations reveal that Cases 1 and 2, which
represent similar overall traffic loads, yield nearly identical
system throughput values across the tested range of TR.

   Based on these observations, a key objective of our study is
to identify the optimal PRACH configuration, specifically the
value of TR, that results in the maximum overflight system
throughput for a given set of traffic conditions, defined by NMt
and pk.
