SUMMARY RECOMMENDATION FOR PROPOSED RESEARCH

To address the limitations identified across works [1]­[13], a satellite-aware Q-learning controller is required. This agent must take
input from (Offered Traffic, Elevation Angle, Satellite Pass Time) to dynamically issue joint (ACB, BI, NPRACH) action decisions, explicitly
penalizing true contention collisions while decoupling Doppler/delay physical detection misses.
